# Viability memo

**Claim:** one operator with a desk like this can carry customer experience, internal automation, the cadence and product feedback for a seven-person payments-infrastructure company through its first design partners.

**Why it is plausible**
- Runtime's own pitch is that ops teams build agents for their procedures with approvals and stored runs ([runtm.com](https://www.runtm.com)). The ops hire using the same product internally is the cheapest proof of it.
- Rollout starts with one SOP and one queue ([Enterprise](https://www.runtm.com/enterprise)); the 14-day plan and the Operate ladder follow that shape.
- Illustrative payback in the Automation Map: the top five internal agents save about 11 hours a week and pay back in about 4.5 weeks at a \$75/h loaded cost. *(Illustrative data; every input is editable on the page.)*

**Risks**
- Real thresholds and procedures differ per partner bank; runbooks need expert review.
- Health signals depend on data the product may not expose yet (cost per case, overrides).
- One person is a single point of failure; agent ownership must be handed off by day 90.

**What would change my mind:** measured hours saved under half of the estimate after four weeks, or design partners not reaching a first approved run within the first week.
