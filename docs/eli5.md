# Explain it like I'm five

Runtime makes helpers that do boring payment chores, like finding a lost payout, and they always ask a grown-up before touching money.

Runtime is hiring one person to look after its customers, make helpers for its own office chores, keep score every week, and tell the builders what is broken.

This website is that person's desk. One drawer picks which office chores to hand to helpers first. One checks on each customer. One holds the recipes for payment chores. One grades the helpers' homework. One writes the weekly report. The numbers are pretend, and it says so.
